# Networking Target List: Physical AI / Warehouse Automation

A starting list of people and organizations to follow, reach out to, and build relationships with over the 3-month sprint. The goal is 30+ outreach messages and 5+ real conversations.

## Categories and examples

### 1. AMR and warehouse automation vendors

Focus on people in product, deployment, solutions, and go-to-market roles.

- Locus Robotics: founders, VP deployment, solutions engineers, APAC leads.
- 6 River Systems / Ocado: deployment, solutions, commercial leads.
- AutoStore: partner managers, APAC expansion, business development.
- Exotec: solutions engineers, deployment leads, APAC commercial.
- Symbotic: deployment, customer success, commercial strategy (heavily US-centric, harder to reach).
- Zebra / Fetch Robotics: fleet management, enterprise solutions.
- MiR / OTTO Motors: industrial AMR deployment leads.
- Geek+: APAC solutions and deployment teams.
- GreyOrange: deployment and solutions leads.

### 2. Warehouse automation integrators and consultants

These people see deployments across vendors and geographies. They are valuable reality checks.

- Dematic / KION: solution design, APAC business development.
- Swisslog / KUKA: warehouse automation consultants and project managers.
- Vanderlande: systems integration and operations consulting.
- Knapp: deployment and software platform leads.
- SSI Schaefer: large-scale warehouse automation integrators.
- Independent consultants and former SI project managers active on LinkedIn and X.

### 3. 3PL and logistics operators

Operators in Southeast Asia are the best source of deployment reality. Target operations directors, warehouse managers, and automation leads.

- Regional 3PLs: CJ Logistics, DHL Supply Chain, DB Schenker, Kuehne+Nagel, Nippon Express, Kerry Logistics, Ninja Van, Janio, Locad.
- E-commerce fulfillment: Lazada, Shopee, Zalora, TikTok Shop logistics teams.
- Cold chain and food distribution: your existing network in Indonesia is a strong wedge here.
- Industrial real estate and logistics parks: Mapletree, GLP, Prologis APAC teams.

### 4. Robotics and Physical AI founders and investors

Build relationships with people who are building or funding Physical AI in Asia and globally.

- VCs with robotics / supply chain focus: Eclipse Ventures, Construct Capital, Basis Set, Lux Capital, Toyota Ventures, AI Fund, Vertex Ventures SE Asia, Openspace Ventures, East Ventures.
- Founders of robotics companies in Southeast Asia or adjacent markets: Weston Robot, LionsBot, SESTO Robotics, HOPE Technik, Movel AI, Alphabot.
- Angel investors and scouts focused on industrial tech and supply chain in Singapore.

### 5. Research and community hubs

- NUS / NTU / SUTD robotics labs and professors working on manipulation, navigation, or logistics automation.
- Singapore Economic Development Board (EDB) and Enterprise Singapore teams covering robotics and supply chain.
- AI Engineer Singapore organizers and members with Physical AI or robotics interest.
- ROS / robotics meetup organizers in Singapore, Jakarta, Bangkok, Ho Chi Minh City.
- Conference speakers from LogiMAT, CeMAT SE Asia, MODEX, ProMat, and ICRA.

### 6. Operator and founder peers

- Other former operators turned robotics investors or advisors.
- CEOs and COOs of 3PLs and e-commerce fulfillment companies in Southeast Asia.
- Supply chain and operations executives from your prior network (GudangAda, BCG, MIT Sloan alumni).

## How to find the right contact

1. Start with the company or organization page on LinkedIn.
2. Search for people with titles like "Solutions Engineer," "Deployment Manager," "Field Operations," "Customer Success," "Product Manager," "APAC Director," or "Business Development."
3. On X, search for people tweeting about warehouse automation, AMR deployments, logistics, or Physical AI.
4. Look for conference speaker lists from LogiMAT, CeMAT SE Asia, and ProMat Asia.
5. Ask warm intros from your existing network before cold outreach.

## Tracking

Use a simple spreadsheet or note file. For each person, track:
- Name, role, company, date of first contact.
- Channel (LinkedIn, X, email, intro).
- Why this person matters.
- Status (identified, contacted, responded, call scheduled, spoke).
- Notes from the conversation.

## Goal

- 20 outreach messages sent.
- 10 responses.
- 5 real conversations (video call or in-person coffee).
