# Outreach Message Templates

Templates for LinkedIn and X outreach. Each message is short, specific, and starts with a real observation. Avoid generic connection requests.

This track is Parked. Keep these templates as reference material until a validated portfolio case is approved for external communication.

## Template 1: To a vendor deployment or solutions person

Hi [Name], I saw your team at [Company] deployed [specific project or milestone]. I am spending the next few months understanding how warehouse automation actually gets deployed in Southeast Asia, especially the gap between the pilot and production. Would you be open to a 15-minute call? I am happy to share notes from the operator side.

## Template 2: To an integrator or consultant

Hi [Name], your work at [Company] across multiple AMR vendors and warehouse types is rare. I am writing a deployment playbook for mid-market 3PLs in Southeast Asia. I would value a 15-minute call on what you see fail most often after go-live. Happy to share any notes I compile.

## Template 3: To a 3PL or logistics operator

Hi [Name], I ran distribution operations in Indonesia and saw the gap between warehouse automation demos and real floor conditions. I am now building expertise in Physical AI deployment. Would you be open to a short call to compare notes on what is actually working in your network?

## Template 4: To a robotics founder or investor

Hi [Name], I have been following [Company / fund] and your focus on [specific area]. I am building a foundation in Physical AI deployment, anchored in warehouse and logistics automation in Southeast Asia. Would you be open to a brief call? I am particularly interested in [deployment / go-to-market / integration challenge].

## Template 5: To a researcher or professor

Hi [Name], I read your recent work on [specific paper or project]. I am a former operator turned Physical AI practitioner, focused on the gap between lab robotics and real warehouse deployment. Would you be open to a 20-minute call on where you see sim-to-real and fleet coordination heading?

## Template 6: To a community organizer or event lead

Hi [Name], I am organizing a small Singapore reading group on Physical AI deployment and operations. I saw your work with [community / event]. Would you be open to a short call to compare notes on format and audience? I would love to co-host if there is a fit.

## Template 7: Follow-up after no response

Hi [Name], I sent a note last week about [specific topic]. I know you are busy. If a call is too much, one sentence on [specific question, e.g., "the biggest integration surprise in your last deployment"] would be valuable. Either way, thank you.

## Template 8: After a call or conversation

Hi [Name], thank you for the time. Three things I noted:
- [specific insight 1]
- [specific insight 2]
- [specific insight 3]

I will follow up with [specific thing you promised]. Let me know if anything from your side changes. I would like to keep in touch as this space evolves.

## Principles

1. Lead with a specific observation, not a generic intro.
2. Ask for 15-20 minutes, not an hour.
3. Offer something concrete in return (notes, operator perspective, Southeast Asia context).
4. Do not pitch yourself as a candidate or vendor.
5. Follow up once. If no response, move on.
